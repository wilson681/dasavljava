# Working rules for this project

## Report in Chinese

All reports and status updates to the user must be written in Chinese (中文向我汇报). This applies to progress updates, summaries, findings, and any explanation of what was done or found — not just bug reports.

## Bug fixes require approval before editing

When a bug is found or reported in this project, always stop and report first:

1. **原因(Cause)** — the root cause, traced through the actual code (what's wrong and why).
2. **修改方案(Proposed fix)** — the specific change proposed, what it does, and why it's the right fix.
3. **建议(Recommendation)** — your recommendation, if there's a judgment call involved (e.g. multiple valid fixes, scope tradeoffs).

Then wait for the user's explicit review and approval before editing any file. Do not jump straight to making the change once a bug is confirmed — not even when the fix looks small or obviously correct, and not even if the user approved a similar fix earlier in the same session. Approval is per-fix, not a standing grant.
